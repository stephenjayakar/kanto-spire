Kanto Spire: privacy

Kanto Spire is a small, private, non-commercial fan game. Playing it requires signing in with Google, and only invited accounts can play.

What we store

  • Your Google account's email address, name and profile picture URL, to sign you in and check that you are invited.
  • Your game data: save file, unlocks, trainer name, finished runs and a log of how each run went (battles, picks, purchases), used to show records and to balance the game.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use it for anything except running this game.
  • We only ask Google for your basic profile and email; we can't see anything else in your account.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Convex (the game's backend). The game itself is hosted on ChatGPT Sites.

Deleting your data

Email aj12ay@gmail.com and your account and game data will be deleted.
